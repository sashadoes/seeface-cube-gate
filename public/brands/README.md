# Brand logos

Put a brand's logo here **only when the brand has agreed** to appear in
seeface1. `starbucks.svg`: the owner confirmed on 2026-10-07 that Starbucks
agreed to this promo. Never take logos from
Wikipedia or the web: they are trademarks and their use needs the owner's
permission.

To turn a logo on: add the file here (PNG or SVG with a transparent background,
square, about 512 × 512), set `logo: "<file name>"` and `licensed: true` on the
campaign in `src/brands/campaigns.ts`, and deploy. The logo then shows on the
corridor posters and over the café counter.
